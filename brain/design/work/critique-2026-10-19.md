# Ledger critique, 2026-10-19 (synthesis)

Two independent critics reviewed the shipped prototype from source (not run): `logging-flows/logging-flows.md` (onboarding, home, add-entry, receipt-scan, review) and `money-views/money-views.md` (activity, accounts, insights, budgets-and-goals, chat). This file ranks their findings together. The five top Blockers were re-verified in code by the orchestrator.

**Totals.** Blockers 9 · Major 15 · Minor 21 · Questions 6. States coverage: 23 of 38 high-priority states handled.

## Verdict
Logging works and feels right: one line becomes an entry with no required decisions, and missing fields fall through to Review as D-0005 intends. Trust fails on the way back out. The same number is computed two ways and shown side by side, an entry can vanish during the "Reading" animation, bills to capitalised merchants are filed as transfers that never touch today's number, and a routine edit can flip an entry's sign and move a balance twice. Fix order: (1) commit before Reading, (2) pick one daily formula, (3) preserve category and sign on edit, (4) stop the transfer regex eating merchants, (5) surface storage failure.

## Major themes (15; details in the two feature files)
- **Numbers that do not reconcile.** Unfiled spend becomes "Other" in Insights, Budgets and chat but Activity's Other filter excludes it; account IN/OUT ignore transfers while ENTRIES and the balance include them; filtered Activity sum is a signed all-inclusive sum, unlike NET; Insights Monthly uses the raw budget while Home and the ring use the suggested fallback; `daysLogged` keys by UTC day.
- **Irreversible money moves without scope.** Transfer legs are unlinked and the delete confirm is silent about the partner; balance edit overwrites with no adjustment entry; refund on an income entry is ignored by `isIncome`; doubt-free receipts auto-log with no undo at the point of logging.
- **Wrong-but-complete entries have no one-tap fix.** "grab food" files as Transport; ONE QUESTION offers three accounts, no Skip, never dismisses; chat "buy" without a price invents ₱65,000 and a "Laptop" goal.
- **Copy the code does not keep.** Home's no-budget hint promises "Salary 45,000 to BPI … M8 sets up the rest" but the budget only derives from `profile.income`; chat refuses offline although M8 is local (new evidence against the D-0001/D-0002 copy); "waiting to sync" when nothing syncs.
